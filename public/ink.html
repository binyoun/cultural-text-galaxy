Lift your ink from the paper

Removes the paper from a photo of your handwriting and keeps only the ink, ready for TouchDesigner. Your photo stays on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Ink colour

For the best result
  • Plain light paper, dark pen or marker
  • Photograph from straight above, in even light
  • If parts of your writing disappear, slide Ink strength towards "more ink"
  • If shadows or paper texture show, slide towards "less ink"
